Marlowe Court Photo Club · issue 14 · Saturday 10 October 2026, 8am

Meet outside Bread & Butter — Ines saves the burnt-edge croissants for the club. Walk down to the Hollis Street flea market while the stalls are still unpacking; the bunting looks best before nine. Follow the towpath to the lock at Fenner’s Bridge for reflections, then climb up to the Block C rooftop garden for the skyline. We finish at Kino Lumière, when the marquee bulbs come on at dusk.

Bring one roll of 400, a spare battery and a flask. Shoot from the hip.
