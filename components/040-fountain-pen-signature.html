Counterpart Lease

This Lease is made the seventh day of October, two thousand and twenty-six, between Aurelia Property Holdings Limited (“the Landlord”) and Adaeze Okonkwo (“the Tenant”).

In witness whereof the Tenant has signed this lease as a deed.
